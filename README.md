## What actually matters when choosing IPTV kids channels?

A huge channel number looks impressive on a pricing page, but parents usually care about a few very practical things.

First, **channel variety**. Kids do not all watch the same thing. Preschoolers, school-age children, and older kids tend to have very different viewing habits. A useful IPTV service should make it easy to move between live children's programming and on-demand content rather than forcing everyone to search through thousands of unrelated channels.

Second, **an EPG guide**. TiviLive says its plans include a full electronic programme guide, so you can see what is airing instead of playing the classic “click, wait, click again” IPTV game. Its FAQ also states that EPG support works with major IPTV players.

Third, **device flexibility**. TiviLive currently lists compatibility with Smart TVs, Firestick, Android, iPhone/iPad, Windows, Mac, MAG devices, Kodi and other IPTV players.

And then there is the family-specific issue: **multiple screens**. One child watching cartoons while someone else watches sports is a lot more realistic than pretending the entire household will agree on one program. TiviLive offers plans from one through five simultaneous connections.

## IPTV kids channels and parental controls

This is the part that can easily get overlooked.

A large IPTV channel library can contain thousands of categories beyond children's programming. For a family device, the **player app's parental-control features** may therefore matter just as much as the provider's channel count.

TiviLive's own IPTV player guide notes that TiviMate supports parental controls, along with features such as custom channel groups and EPG organization.
